Photography

Pacific Northwest street, environmental portraits, and natural light — shot on a Canon Rebel T7i and modular cage rig.

Approach

I focus on the fundamentals: reading ambient light, understanding exposure, and framing clean geometry before lifting the camera. Most of my work centers on the Pacific Northwest — rain-slicked streets, moody coastal light, and candid environmental portraits.

I work with available light and handle exposure in-camera: RAW capture, deliberate framing, and an active histogram.

Field Kit

  • Camera & Rig Canon EOS Rebel T7i  ·  Neewer Aluminum Cage Rig
  • Lenses 50mm Prime (portraits & low light)  ·  18–135mm IS STM (all-around focal range)
